# Rev3 rail switches

Generated by `rails.py`. Do not edit by hand; change the script and re-run it.

One TPS48111-Q1 eFuse per rail (two for the 42 V outputs), with a resettable PTC on each 24 / 12 / 5 V output. All parts are leaded for hand soldering. Load capacitances are **assumed** until the real loads are known.

- **42V-D:** EPOS4 drive group: two EPOS4 50/5 per connector, two connectors in use and two spare (ASSUMED 470 uF per EPOS4). The 50 A / 0.5 s trip must sit above the drives' acceleration peaks. The E-STOP loop feeds this driver's EN/UVLO, so opening it cuts motor power in hardware without touching the Orin. Defaults OFF: the drives are useless without a controller.
- **42V-C:** Converter-feed group: three feeds plus one spare. 24 V 300 W + 12 V 150 W + 5 V 150 W is about 22 A at 30 V; the 30 A design leaves room for a converter on the spare. Defaults ON, because the 12 V converter powers the Orin.
- **12V:** Feeds the Jetson AGX Orin, so it defaults ON in hardware.
- **54V:** The eFuse sits on the boost's 42 V input. It switches the boost and is its short-circuit protection, because a boost cannot block current from input to output. Defaults ON: the Zyxel is the rover's main network switch. C_LOAD includes the boost's output side and the Zyxel's input: ASSUMED 600 uF.

## Per-output PTCs

A PTC protects the cable to one device from an overload too small to trip the rail. Each output's cable must carry the PTC's trip current continuously. Fit a bigger PTC on any output whose device needs more than the hold current.

| Rail | Outputs | PTC (JLC #) | V max | Hold / trip | Drop at hold | Cable at least |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24V | 8 x Deans T | SMD2920P300TF/33 (C2943634, 2920) | 33 V | 3 / 6 A | >= 36 mV | 0.5 mm2 / AWG 20 |
| 12V | 7 x XT60 | SMD2920P300TF/16 (C167318, 2920) | 16 V | 3 / 6 A | >= 36 mV | 0.5 mm2 / AWG 20 |
| 5V | 11 x XT30 | SMD1812P300TF (C21004, 1812) | 8 V | 3 / 5 A | >= 30 mV | 0.5 mm2 / AWG 20 |

The 42 V outputs have no PTC. Their cables are heavy, and no SMD PTC is rated for 42 V at drive currents. Use XT90-S anti-spark plugs on the 42 V devices, so hot-plugging onto the live group cannot trip its short-circuit detector.

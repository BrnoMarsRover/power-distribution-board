## Rail budget

Per-output limits protect each device and its cable. They are not meant to sum to the rail: when several outputs run near their limits together, the converter's own current limit acts fast and the firmware sheds outputs by priority on a slower, thermal time scale.

| Rail | Outputs | Limit each | Sum of limits | Rail design current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42V | 8 | 15.9 A | 127 A | 40.0 A |
| 24V | 8 | 5.0 A | 40 A | 12.5 A |
| 15V | 8 | 7.9 A | 63 A | 20.0 A |
| 12V | 7 | 7.9 A | 55 A | 25.0 A |
| 5V | 11 | 4.0 A | 44 A | 30.0 A |
